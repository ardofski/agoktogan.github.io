I am a Computer Science M.S. student at **Bilkent University**, with an expected graduation in **January 2027**. My research focuses on **3D scene reconstruction and completion, neural rendering, and generative world models**. I work with **Ayşegül Dündar and Duygu Ceylan** on geometry-aware scene completion and camera optimization for generative inpainting.

I contributed to **StyleFusion360 (ECCV 2026)** through experiments on multiview consistency and style interpolation, and by implementing masking for localized style transfer. My earlier research includes reference-based image editing, Turkish NLP with Deniz Yuret at Koç University AI Lab, and scientific visualization with Sedat Ozer at Bilkent University.

I received my B.S. in Computer Science from Bilkent University in 2022, with a **3.85/4.00 GPA (8/160)**. From 2022 to 2025, I worked as a Computer Vision Engineer at ArgosAI on video understanding and real-time inference for airport runway safety.

I am interested in Ph.D. opportunities in 3D computer vision, neural rendering, and generative models.
