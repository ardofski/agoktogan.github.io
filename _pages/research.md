## StyleFusion360

**View-Consistent Head Stylization via Adaptive Style Modulation · ECCV 2026**

StyleFusion360 generates stylized 3D heads with a consistent appearance across viewpoints from a style reference image.

**My contributions**

- Conducted shuffled-prediction experiments in a multiview diffusion model to study consistency across prediction batches.
- Investigated interpolation for combining multiple styles.
- Implemented masking in the proposed attention mechanism for localized style transfer.

## Geometry-Aware 3D Scene Completion

**M.S. research · Bilkent University DLRLab · September 2025–present**  
**Advisors:** [Ayşegül Dündar](https://www.cs.bilkent.edu.tr/~adundar/) and [Duygu Ceylan](https://www.duygu-ceylan.com/)

I study how to select informative views for completing partially observed 3D scenes. My work uses differentiable camera optimization to determine where an inpainting model should generate new content within generative world-model pipelines.

## Refedit

**Zero-Shot Reference-Based Image Editing · Bilkent University · 2024–2025**  
**With:** [Ayşegül Dündar](https://www.cs.bilkent.edu.tr/~adundar/)

I developed reference-based object insertion with automatic edit localization, without model fine-tuning, paired training data, or a user-provided mask. The project explored latent-space refinement to balance style transfer, background preservation, and reference fidelity.

## Mukayese: Turkish NLP Strikes Back

**Findings of ACL 2022 · Koç University AI Lab**  
**Advisor:** Deniz Yuret

I contributed data preparation, evaluation, and training baselines for part-of-speech tagging, named entity recognition, and spell checking in a Turkish NLP benchmark. I also developed a Hunspell-based Turkish spell checker for LibreOffice.

## Multiscale Ocean Eddy Analysis

**IEEE VIS 2020 · SciVis Contest Paper · Honorable Mention**  
**Research with:** Sedat Ozer and collaborators

I implemented optical flow on volumetric ocean data to study eddies at multiple scales. The resulting contest paper, *The Use of 3D Optical Flow, Feature-Tracking and Token-Tracking Petri Nets to Analyze and Visualize Multiple Scales of Ocean Eddies*, received an Honorable Mention at the IEEE SciVis Contest 2020.
